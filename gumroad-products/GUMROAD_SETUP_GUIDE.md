## STEP 1: Create Your Account (5 minutes)

1. Go to **gumroad.com**
2. Click **"Start Selling"** (big button)
3. Choose **"I want to sell on Gumroad"**
4. Sign up with:
   - Email address (use your business email)
   - Password
   - Or "Sign up with Google" (easiest)
5. Verify your email (check inbox, click link)
6. Choose **"Creator"** when asked what you are

**What you see now:** Gumroad dashboard (empty)

---

## STEP 2: Set Up How You Get Paid (10 minutes)

### IMPORTANT: Do this BEFORE you make sales

1. Click **"Settings"** (gear icon) in left sidebar
2. Click **"Payout Settings"**
3. Choose your country
4. Connect payout method:
   - **PayPal** (fastest - 1-2 days)
   - **Bank Account** (direct deposit - 1 week)
5. Fill in your details:
   - Full legal name
   - Address
   - Tax info (SSN or Tax ID)
   - Date of birth
6. Click **"Save"**

**Gumroad pays out every Friday for sales from the previous week.**

**Minimum payout:** $10 (PayPal) or $50 (bank)

**Fees:** 10% of each sale (Gumroad takes this automatically)

Example: You sell bundle for $497. You get $447.30. Gumroad keeps $49.70.

## STEP 6: Set Up Affiliate Program (5 minutes)

1. Go to **Settings** → **"Advanced"** → **"Affiliates"**
2. Turn ON **"Enable affiliates for your products"**
3. Set commission: **50%**
   - This means if someone sells your bundle for $497, they get $248.50, you get $248.50
4. Set cookie duration: **30 days**
   - If someone clicks an affiliate link and buys within 30 days, the affiliate gets credit
5. Click **Save**

**How affiliates get links:**
- They sign up at: `gumroad.com/affiliates`
- They search for your products
- They get unique tracking links
- Gumroad handles all tracking and payments automatically

## TROUBLESHOOTING

### Problem: "I can't connect payout"
**Fix:** You need to provide tax info. Go to Settings → Payout → fill all fields including SSN/Tax ID.

### Problem: "Upload fails"
**Fix:** File too big? ZIP compresses it. If still too big, remove large files and add a note "Contact me for additional files."

### Problem: "My store URL is ugly"
**Fix:** Go to Settings → Profile → change username to something short like "godai" → URL becomes `gumroad.com/godai`

### Problem: "I don't have a cover image"
**Fix:** Go to canva.com → search "digital product banner" → pick template → customize → download → upload to Gumroad.

### Problem: "Buyers can't find their download"
**Fix:** Gumroad emails download link. Tell buyers to check spam folder. Also available in their Gumroad library at gumroad.com/library.

### Problem: "I want to offer discount codes"
**Fix:** Go to product → "Offer codes" → "Create offer code" → Set code like "EARLYBIRD20" → 20% off.
