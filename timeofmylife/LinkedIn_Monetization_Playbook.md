## CONTENT ENGINE (Weekly — 4 posts + 1 newsletter)

### Monday — Text Post (Framework insight)
Short, punchy, one idea from Volume I. 900–1,600 characters.
First 2 lines must hook before the "see more" fold.

Example:
```
Everyone thinks AI intelligence = parameter count.

That's like saying human intelligence = number of neurons.

Parameter count is one axis. Here are the other 8 that matter:

→ Abstraction depth (vertical scaling)
→ Population interaction (horizontal scaling)
→ Evolutionary generations (temporal scaling)
→ Knowledge access
→ Perception and embodiment
→ Validation and selection
→ Environment richness
→ Inference-time computation

The question isn't "how big is the model?"
It's "what kind of ecology is the model embedded in?"

That's the thesis of Beyond Parameter Scaling.
Full framework in the comments.
```

**Rules:**
- Never put links in the post body. Links in first comment.
- End with a question to drive comments.
- Reply to every comment within the first hour.

## THE GOLDEN RULES

1. **Never put links in post body.** Links in first comment. Posts with links get ~50% less reach.
2. **Reply to every comment in the first hour.** Engagement velocity drives distribution.
3. **80/20 value-to-promotion.** Four posts of pure value for every one that sells something.
4. **Consistency over intensity.** 3 posts/week for 6 months beats 10 posts/week for 2 weeks.
5. **Engagement rate > follower count.** 12K followers at 6% engagement is worth more than 80K at 0.4%.
6. **Lean into video.** Every monetization program LinkedIn is building favors video creators.
7. **Document your process.** "I rendered a 26-shot film on free GPUs — here's what broke" is more engaging than "My toolkit is great."
8. **Own your niche.** You are the AI philosopher-practitioner. No one else is making this argument. That is your moat.
