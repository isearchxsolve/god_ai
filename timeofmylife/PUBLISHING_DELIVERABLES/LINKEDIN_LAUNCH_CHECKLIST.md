# LinkedIn Launch Checklist

## How to use this

Use this like an execution sheet.

Do not overthink.
Do not redesign the strategy.
Do not build new products before finishing the checklist.

Just move top to bottom.

## The core objective

Your goal is not to look impressive.
Your goal is to create:
- conversations
- DMs
- one paid reel
- one PDF sale
- proof you can reuse

## Before day 1

- [ ] Open `PUBLISHING_DELIVERABLES/books/commercial/linkedin/LinkedIn_Monetization_Complete_Tutorial.pdf`
- [ ] Open `PUBLISHING_DELIVERABLES/LINKEDIN_POSTS_AND_DM_SCRIPTS.pdf`
- [ ] Decide your main CTA: `REEL`
- [ ] Decide your product CTA: `LAB`
- [ ] Choose one sample reel
- [ ] Pick your first product: `Free GPU Film Lab`
- [ ] Decide your first service price: `₹1,500`
- [ ] Decide your first PDF price: `₹499`
- [ ] Keep your UPI ID ready
- [ ] Keep your file delivery link ready

## Day 1 — profile setup

- [ ] Update your LinkedIn headline
- [ ] Update your About section
- [ ] Add your best reel to Featured
- [ ] Add `Free GPU Film Lab` to Featured
- [ ] Add one clear banner message if possible
- [ ] Make sure your profile clearly says what you do
- [ ] Make sure your profile clearly says how to contact you

### End-of-day check
- [ ] My profile now supports selling

## Day 2 — service post

- [ ] Publish the service offer post
- [ ] Add first comment telling people to comment `REEL`
- [ ] Reply to every comment fast
- [ ] DM every person who comments `REEL`
- [ ] Send 5 warm DMs manually after posting

### End-of-day check
- [ ] I posted the offer
- [ ] I replied quickly
- [ ] I started conversations

## Day 3 — outbound day

- [ ] Send 10 DMs to founders, creators, or artists
- [ ] Use the ready-made script
- [ ] Personalize the first line only
- [ ] Do not write giant essays
- [ ] Track who replied
- [ ] Follow up with anyone who asked a question

### End-of-day check
- [ ] I sent 10 real messages
- [ ] I did outreach instead of hiding in preparation

## Day 4 — proof post

- [ ] Publish an educational / proof post
- [ ] Explain what most AI video gets wrong
- [ ] Mention identity consistency across shots
- [ ] Soft CTA at the end
- [ ] Answer every comment
- [ ] DM warm responders

### End-of-day check
- [ ] I posted proof, not just an offer

## Day 5 — product post

- [ ] Publish the `Free GPU Film Lab` post
- [ ] Use CTA: `Comment LAB`
- [ ] DM everyone who comments `LAB`
- [ ] Offer the `₹499` product simply
- [ ] Mention value, not hype
- [ ] Ask if they want payment details now

### End-of-day check
- [ ] I put a real product in front of people

## Day 6 — service follow-up

- [ ] Follow up with every `REEL` lead who did not close
- [ ] Offer the intro rate calmly
- [ ] Ask for their page / product / song link
- [ ] Confirm you can suggest one reel idea
- [ ] Post a soft founder-facing offer if needed

### End-of-day check
- [ ] I followed up instead of waiting passively

## Day 7 — weekly review

- [ ] Count total posts published
- [ ] Count total DMs sent
- [ ] Count total replies
- [ ] Count total comments
- [ ] Count total service leads
- [ ] Count total product leads
- [ ] Count total sales
- [ ] Identify which CTA performed better: `REEL` or `LAB`
- [ ] Decide what to repeat next week

### End-of-week question
- [ ] What got the strongest signal?

## Week 2 — repeat with focus

## Day 8
- [ ] Repost service angle from a new perspective
- [ ] Show the reel again or a variation of it
- [ ] Ask for `REEL`

## Day 9
- [ ] Send 10 more outbound DMs
- [ ] Focus only on the best audience from week 1
- [ ] Drop weak audiences

## Day 10
- [ ] Publish a lesson learned post
- [ ] Talk about what actually makes AI video usable
- [ ] Soft CTA to DM or comment

## Day 11
- [ ] Follow up with all warm leads again
- [ ] Use the short follow-up script
- [ ] Close at least one conversation decisively

## Day 12
- [ ] Post `KIT` or bundle offer only if enough interest exists
- [ ] Offer the practical bundle at `₹1,999`
- [ ] Do not overwhelm cold people with every product

## Day 13
- [ ] Ask for testimonial from any buyer or user
- [ ] Publish one case study, result, or behind-the-scenes proof
- [ ] Save the best replies for reuse

## Day 14
- [ ] Review numbers again
- [ ] Keep only the best-performing offer
- [ ] Raise prices slightly if you closed buyers
- [ ] Remove distractions
- [ ] Plan the next 7 days based on evidence

## Daily non-negotiables

Every day, make sure these happen:
- [ ] one concrete action that could lead to money
- [ ] one follow-up
- [ ] one public proof or one direct outreach action
- [ ] no unnecessary redesigning
- [ ] no adding random new offers

## Service sales checklist

Before selling a reel:
- [ ] send the service DM
- [ ] state the price clearly
- [ ] ask for their page / product / song link
- [ ] confirm what the reel should do
- [ ] confirm delivery timeline
- [ ] ask for payment

After payment:
- [ ] collect source material
- [ ] confirm creative direction
- [ ] promise first draft timeline
- [ ] deliver fast
- [ ] ask for revisions in bullet points
- [ ] ask for testimonial after success

## Product sales checklist

Before selling a PDF:
- [ ] say what the product is
- [ ] say who it is for
- [ ] state price clearly
- [ ] ask if they want payment details now

After agreement:
- [ ] send UPI / payment method
- [ ] ask for screenshot
- [ ] send file or access link immediately
- [ ] ask whether they want the bundle too

## Red flags

If any of these happen, correct immediately:
- [ ] I keep making new products instead of reaching out
- [ ] I am posting without a CTA
- [ ] I am waiting for perfect branding
- [ ] I am talking too much in DMs
- [ ] I am trying to sell all books at once
- [ ] I am not following up
- [ ] I am not tracking outcomes

## Metrics sheet

Track these numbers:
- [ ] posts this week
- [ ] outbound DMs sent
- [ ] inbound DMs received
- [ ] comments received
- [ ] `REEL` leads
- [ ] `LAB` leads
- [ ] service closes
- [ ] product closes
- [ ] revenue this week

## Minimum win condition

A successful first launch is not perfection.
A successful first launch is:
- [ ] 1 service conversation
- [ ] 1 product conversation
- [ ] 1 payment
- [ ] 1 piece of proof to reuse next week

## Final reminder

If you freeze, do this:

- [ ] post the service offer
- [ ] ask people to comment `REEL`
- [ ] DM every person who comments
- [ ] pitch `Free GPU Film Lab` to people who want the practical workflow
- [ ] follow up the next day

That is enough to start making LinkedIn useful.
