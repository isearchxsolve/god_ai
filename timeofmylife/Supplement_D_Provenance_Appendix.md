## APPENDIX B — ORIGIN OF THE FRAMEWORK

### Author's note on provenance

The ideas in this book were not developed through a conventional research trajectory
— graduate training, laboratory work, peer-reviewed publication. They originated in
the working practice of a production software engineer who repeatedly arrived at
questions from first principles, sometimes years before encountering the
corresponding formal literature.

This appendix records that history for transparency. It is not offered as evidence for
any claim in the book. A question asked early is not the same as a question answered
correctly. The literature cited in the main text provides the evidentiary foundation;
this appendix provides the biographical context.

### Timeline of key questions

The following is a condensed record of when the author first formulated the central
questions of this project, alongside the approximate date at which the author
encountered relevant published work.

**Multi-dimensional intelligence (P1)**
The intuition that intelligence cannot be reduced to a single metric — whether
parameter count, IQ, or any other scalar — emerged from the author's engineering
experience with systems that performed differently depending on tool access, memory,
environment and interaction structure, not only on model capacity. The formal
literature on scaling laws (Kaplan et al., 2020; Hoffmann et al., 2022) confirmed the
power of parameter scaling while simultaneously suggesting its limits — precisely
the tension that P1 addresses.

**Separation of exploration and validation (P2)**
The principle that generation should be allowed to be broad before evaluation
becomes aggressive came from software engineering practice: build many
candidates, test ruthlessly. The formal precedent — AlphaEvolve's explicit
separation of LLM generation from automated evaluation — was encountered later.

**Analogy as novelty mechanism (P3)**
Cross-domain reasoning as a creative mechanism was a working practice before it
was a hypothesis. The author's creative work (screenplays, philosophical writing,
film production) consistently used cross-domain analogy as a generative technique.
The Webb et al. (2023) finding of emergent analogical reasoning in LLMs and the
Shen et al. (2026) demonstration of diversity gains from explicit analogical reasoning
provided independent validation.

**Contradiction-preserving synthesis (P4)**
The intuition that competing hypotheses should sometimes be held in tension rather
than immediately resolved emerged from the author's experience with creative and
philosophical work that seemed to require holding contradictory positions
simultaneously. The Joker corpus (Volume II) is the creative expression of this
intuition. The formal grounding in paraconsistent logic and argumentation theory
(Supplement A, Part B) was developed during the convergence pass to address the
review finding that P4 lacked formal support.

**Population-level evolution (P5)**
The idea that populations of competing and cooperating agents can produce
solutions that no single agent would discover came from observing evolutionary
dynamics in both software systems and creative communities. XLand (2021) and
AlphaEvolve (2025) provided concrete experimental precedent.

**Horizontal vs. vertical scaling (P6)**
The distinction between making one system wider and making one system deeper
was an engineering intuition before it was a research hypothesis. The field's growing
recognition of this distinction — visible in the NeurIPS 2026 and ICLR 2026
workshops on multi-agent learning and environment scaling — suggests the intuition
is converging with mainstream research.

**Perception and embodiment (P7)**
The question of whether sensory experience changes the character of intelligence
—not just its content — emerged from the author's conviction that text-only
systems, however powerful, might be missing something available to embodied
systems. This remains the most speculative proposition and has been decomposed
into four sub-propositions (Supplement A, Part C) to separate what is established
from what is philosophical.

**Capability and governance (P8)**
The governance hypothesis — that capability and restriction can be separated —
came from practical experience with systems that could be broadly capable while
being externally constrained. The EU AI Act (2024) represents the real-world
implementation of this approach.

**Creator/creation autonomy (P9)**
The philosophical proposition that a creation can exceed its creator is the thread
connecting all three volumes of this project. It appeared first in the creative work
(the Joker as autonomous character), then in the spiritual memoir (the Creator/
Creation relationship across religious traditions), and finally in the research
program (the question of whether a computational ecology can produce emergent
capabilities). It is a philosophical question, not an empirical one, and is presented
as such.

### What this provenance does and does not establish

**It establishes:** That the author arrived at these questions through engineering
practice and first-principles reasoning, and that several of those questions have
since received independent validation from published research.

**It does not establish:** That arriving at a question early confers any special
authority over the answer. That the author's personal timeline is a substitute for
literature review. That engineering intuition is equivalent to scientific evidence.

**The correct reading:** The author's history explains why these particular questions
were asked. The literature cited in the main text determines whether the proposed
answers are defensible. Provenance is context. Evidence is evidence.

### Relationship to the creative corpus

A substantial creative corpus — screenplays, film production documents, memoirs,
philosophical commentary — was produced in parallel with the research ideas.
This corpus is documented in Volume II (The Joker Corpus Dossier). The creative
work and the research work inform each other but should not be presented as the
same kind of evidence. The screenplays are art. The research program is science.
The memoir is phenomenology. Each has its own standards of validation.

### Acknowledgment

This framework draws on independently developed research across multiple fields.
No proposition in this book is claimed as a priority discovery. The contribution
sought is the synthesis — the insistence that these independently validated
components (multidimensional scaling, evolutionary discovery, analogical reasoning,
separation of generation from validation, population-level emergence) might be
organized into a coherent research architecture and tested as a system.

The scientific community is invited to reproduce, criticize, formalize, extend and
falsify.
