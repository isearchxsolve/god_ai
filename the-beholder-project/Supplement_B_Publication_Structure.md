## PROPOSED PUBLICATION ARCHITECTURE

The project is a unified body of work with three distinct facets. It should be published
as **three volumes** with clear boundaries, shared references, and no duplicated text.

```
THE BEHOLDER PROJECT
├── Volume I — Beyond Parameter Scaling
│   The scientific research program
│
├── Volume II — The Joker Corpus Dossier
│   The creative-archive evidence base
│
└── Volume III — The Beholder Between Fullness and Emptiness
    The spiritual-philosophical memoir
```

### Volume I — Beyond Parameter Scaling (Canonical Research Document)

**What it contains:**
- The V2 Convergence pass as the primary text (9 propositions, Omega architecture,
  falsifiable experimental program, final establishment)
- The expanded literature base from Supplement A (48 references)
- The formal grounding for P4 from Supplement A (paraconsistent logic, AGMp, ADFs)
- The decomposed P7 from Supplement A (P7a–P7d)
- The V1 material **rewritten** as a single consolidated Appendix A: "Origin of the
  Framework" — not a chapter-by-chapter reproduction but a condensed historical
  narrative explaining how the framework developed
- The provenance timeline consolidated into Appendix B (see below)
- The mathematical core (scaling elasticity, interaction synergy, etc.) retained as
  Appendix C

**What it does NOT contain:**
- The full V1 text as a separate document (condensed into Appendix A)
- Any spiritual or memoir material (that belongs in Volume III)
- Any creative-archive analysis (that belongs in Volume II)

**Structure:**
```
Volume I — BEYOND PARAMETER SCALING
  Executive Summary
```

## DEDUPLICATION RULES

1. **No text appears in more than one volume.** Cross-references use citations:
   "For the formal research program, see Volume I, Part 2."
2. **V1 material is condensed, not deleted.** The full V1 text exists in three files
   currently. It should be condensed into a single Appendix A in Volume I that
   preserves the key ideas and historical narrative without reproducing the full
   chapter-by-chapter text.
3. **The Omega text is published once** — in Volume I. Volume III references it.
4. **The provenance timeline is published once** — in Volume I, Appendix B.
5. **The Research and Source Discipline note is published once** — in Volume III,
   at the front.

## NAMING CONVENTION

The unified project should have a single name that encompasses all three volumes.
Suggested names:

- **The Beholder Project** (emphasizes the observing/witnessing framework)
- **The Omega Corpus** (emphasizes the research program)
- **Beyond Parameter Scaling: A Trilogy** (emphasizes the scientific thesis)

The name should be chosen before publication. Each volume should carry the series
name on its title page.
